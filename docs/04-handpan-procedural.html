Step-Level Grammar Enforcement with Probability Tolerance

At step (bar, s), the grammar assigns a soft constraint state G(bar, s) with a tolerance inversely tied to entropy. All candidate pitches j ∈ {0..8} remain in the feasible space, but grammatical obligations add strong negative energy (attraction) or positive energy (repulsion):

           + E_motif(j; dna[s], mut)     // pulls toward live-recorded motif DNA (weakened by mutation)
