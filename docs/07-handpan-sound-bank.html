IV. Empirical Spectral Findings from Yishama Virtual Pantam

Why Naive Synths Sound "Plastic" vs. Real Yishama Pantam Acoustics:
  • Nonlinear Modal Bloom: In real Yishama recordings, 2f₀ and 3f₀ do not peak at t = 0. Via geometric plate nonlinearity, energy transfers from f₀ into 2f₀/3f₀ over 100–220 ms (measured bloom ratio 1.5×–2.8× between 30 ms and 140 ms).
  • Inverted Decay Hierarchy (τ₃ > τ₂ > τ₁): The fundamental radiates strongly into air/cavity (τ₁ ≈ 0.41–0.50 s), while higher quadrupole/hexapole modes radiate less and ring longer (τ₂ ≈ 0.50–0.80 s, τ₃ ≈ 0.85–1.25 s), surpassing f₀ in the sustain tail (+1 to +6 dB).
  • Clean Low-Mid Skin Contact: Broadband non-harmonic impact energy vanishes within 5–18 ms and sits in the warm low-mid band; energy above 4 kHz is < −35 dB (eliminating synthetic metallic hiss).
  • Coupled Beating & Helmholtz Cavity: Slow 0.55–2.2 Hz orthogonal-axis beating creates organic breathing shimmer, paired with a 70–86 Hz cavity mode on Ding/low notes (−16 to −26 dB) and stereo correlation 0.91–0.98.
