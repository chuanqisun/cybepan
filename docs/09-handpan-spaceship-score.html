Architecture & Research Notes

1. Spaceship Visual Discipline

Adapted from chuanqisun/spaceship: pure obsidian #000000, a 5-step white ink hierarchy, monospace tabular numerals, and a single electric signal color (#ff5a1f) reserved exclusively for what is actively changing right now — cooling smoothly over ~450ms back into crisp monochrome ink.

2. Unified Score = Instrument Surface

Replaces the literal circular handpan drawing with an Elastic Spatial Score where Y encodes the 9 scale degrees (Ding ^1 bottom to ^5' top, plus rim Tak × and cavity Gu ◇) and X encodes real physical time. Clicking or gliding across lanes strikes with <5ms latency, injects your note into the living score, and entrains the engine's tempo and harmony (Comparator Agency).

3. Organic Tempo (Todd + KTH Rubato)

Eliminates rigid eighth-note metronome grids. Every inter-onset interval is synthesized continuously from Todd phrase-arch kinematics (accelerando toward phrase apex, expressive ritardando into cadence), KTH breath caesuras (luftpause +25%–75% after wide leaps or cadences), agogic root lingering, and tight 40–65ms grace cascades that visibly cluster on the score.

4. Continuous Attention & Vector Steering

Replaces discrete Micro/Short/Mid/Long sliders with continuous SVG Bezier attention arcs from influential past notes directly to the playhead — arc span naturally encodes temporal distance while stroke weight encodes attention pull. Click any past note or drag-sweep 2–6 notes to Lock Attention onto that motif.

Drag the tethered HARM / TIME knobs (or pull right from the Playhead) to open the Radial Graduated Protractor Fan HUD with physical ruler ticks drawn along the tether line and dry stereo-panned mechanical detent micro-clicks.

5. PanLang & Fast Gemini Decisions

Compresses live musical memory into a hyper-dense symbolic grammar (e.g., DKurd @i ~76 | 0: 4-6 4: , | @bVI *2: 6-8 t | LOCK: 4-6-8) paired with a tight 3-question suite (next_move, harmonic_color, focal_deg) via v1beta/decisions and instant AbortController cancellation whenever you scrub backward to fork history.
